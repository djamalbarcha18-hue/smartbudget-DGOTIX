# Destination pictures

One picture per place in `assets/data/destinations.json`, named after its
`id`: `mecca.webp`, `medina.webp`, `bangkok.webp`, `london.webp`,
`istanbul.webp`, `dubai.webp`, `paris.webp`, `kuala_lumpur.webp`, `rome.webp`,
`barcelona.webp`, `marrakech.webp`, `cairo.webp`, `bali.webp`, `tunis.webp`,
`tbilisi.webp`.

- Format: WebP, square, about 256 × 256 px, under 40 KB each.
- A place without its picture shows its icon instead, so they can be added
  one at a time.
- Use only pictures you may reuse in an app (your own, or a licence that
  allows it), and record each one below.
