SmartBudget helps you see where your money goes and keep it on plan, in a clear app that works even offline.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Track income and expenses in seconds, or type them the way you'd say them: "coffee 4.5; taxi 12 yesterday; salary 3800".
• Scan a receipt: the app reads the shop, date, amount and items, and you review before saving.
• Set a monthly budget per category and watch what is left.
• Wallets for cash, bank accounts and e-wallets, in any currency, with transfers and balance checks.
• Savings goals, debts and loans, recurring bills and salaries, added on their date.
• Seasonal budgets for Ramadan, Eid, back to school and holidays.
• Savings circles: who collects when, what you paid, what is late.
• A financial health score that explains what helps and what hurts.
• Monthly, quarterly and yearly reports, and PDF export.
• Zakat calculator with the lunar-year countdown.
• Live exchange rates, gold and crypto prices.
• DGOTIX AI: ask about your spending in plain language.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Everything is saved on your phone first. Sign in to sync between your phone and computer, or keep it on this device only. No ads, no selling data. Add an app lock with a PIN or your fingerprint, and export a full backup any time.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE AND CURRENCY
Arabic, English, French, Indonesian, Spanish, Turkish, Malay and Urdu, with 40+ currencies. Dark and light mode.
